# The Highest Loop — swyx

## Summary
swyx gives a short opening keynote for the AI Engineer World's Fair. It is part framing idea, part conference welcome, and it sets "loops" as the lens for the day's talks on automations and software factories.

**A history told as loops**
"In the beginning, there was the token." Then chat, then tools, then goals, and now automations, cron jobs and loops, with "a lot of loop stacking." He points to his essay on loop craft and a workshop on it the day before.

**The core skill: moving between loops**
He calls it "basically a core skill of AI engineer" to define which loop you are working in and judge whether it is the right level of abstraction. When you are ready, go up a loop to reach the next level of productivity and scale your inference. When you find issues, go down a loop to work on reliability and fix bugs. His simpler analogy for programmers: it is "a stack of wild loops." He tells attendees who complain about the many tracks that he will not apologize for the content; they must route themselves to the level they work at or are blocked on.

**The human loop**
He maps the same pattern onto human life: heartbeat, talking, tools, work (eat, sleep, code, repeat), then teams and families. He has promoted "tiny teams" before and says they matter more and more. Beyond that sits the broader project of civilization and collective intelligence. His point: what we learn about loops in coding is crossing over into human life.

**AI beyond coding**
Repeating his last keynote's message that agents built for one domain are generalizing, he notes this year's new vertical tracks (healthcare, finance, go-to-market) and a forward-deployed engineering track, all signs that AI is "diffusing finally beyond coding."

**Growth and the highest loop**
Attendance grew from the first event in 2023 to about 2,000 at the first World's Fair in 2024, and to over 7,000 this year against a 6,000 target, with ticket buying still skewed late. The "highest loop" is where humans come together to figure out what the next loop is: "the loop that makes loops." That is why the team runs summits in New York, Paris, Singapore, Melbourne and Miami, invites partners to start more, and calls the World's Fair the "summit of summits."

## Why it matters
- Offers a compact mental model for agent work: name the loop you are in, go up a loop for scale and down a loop for reliability.
- The nested-while-loop framing gives a simple vocabulary for layering cron jobs, agent loops and software factories.
- Records the conference's own signal that AI is spreading past coding, through vertical tracks and forward-deployed engineering.
- Useful as an opening frame for a chapter on loops or automation, with short, quotable lines such as the loop that makes loops.
